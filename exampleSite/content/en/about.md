**Orbitline** is a fictional product. The posts, authors and numbers on this site are sample content that exists to show the layout.

The site uses the **Gridline** theme: a gray-white canvas, hairline construction lines, serif display type, mono metadata and a single electric-blue accent.

## What the theme includes

- A blog index with a grid hero, three featured cards, a category rail and a compact post list
- Post pages with author avatars, reading time, an "On this page" rail and a "See also" box
- Code blocks with a language label and copy button, Markdown alerts, and captioned image frames
- Light and dark modes; English and Traditional Chinese interface strings

> [!TIP]
> Narrow the window below 1080px on any post and the table of contents folds into a disclosure at the top of the article.
